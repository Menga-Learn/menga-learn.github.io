## Relazione con raccomandazioni

La tua azienda ha fatto un sondaggio tra i 200 dipendenti sulla mensa aziendale. Risultati principali:

- 62% la trova troppo cara;
- 48% vorrebbe più opzioni vegetariane;
- 35% non la usa perché gli orari (12:00–13:30) non sono compatibili con le riunioni;
- 80% apprezza la qualità del personale.

Scrivi una **relazione formale** per la direzione (**220–260 parole**) con titoli, che presenti i risultati e proponga **tre raccomandazioni** motivate.

### Una relazione modello (248 parole)

> **Staff Canteen Survey: Findings and Recommendations**
>
> **Introduction**
> The aim of this report is to summarise the results of the recent staff survey on the company canteen and to propose measures to increase its use. All 200 employees were invited to participate.
>
> **Main findings**
> The canteen staff were highly rated, with 80% of respondents praising their friendliness and efficiency. However, a clear majority (62%) consider the prices excessive, while almost half would welcome a wider range of vegetarian dishes. Moreover, roughly a third of employees reported that they rarely use the canteen because its opening hours clash with meetings scheduled around midday.
>
> **Recommendations**
> In light of these findings, the following measures are recommended:
> 1. *Pricing*: introducing a reduced-price "daily menu" would address the most common complaint without significantly affecting overall revenue.
> 2. *Menu*: at least two vegetarian main courses should be available every day. This could be trialled for three months and its impact monitored.
> 3. *Opening hours*: extending service until 14:30 would allow staff with late-morning meetings to use the facility. Alternatively, managers could be asked to avoid scheduling meetings between 12:30 and 13:30.
>
> **Conclusion**
> While the canteen is clearly valued for the quality of its service, its cost and limited flexibility are deterring a significant number of staff. Implementing the measures above would, in all likelihood, lead to a noticeable increase in usage.

### Cosa notare

- **Titoli** e linguaggio **impersonale** (*it is recommended*, *respondents*).
- Dati riformulati con **quantificatori precisi** (*a clear majority, almost half, roughly a third*).
- Ogni raccomandazione è **collegata a un dato** e motivata.
- Conclusione che **valuta** senza introdurre nuove informazioni.
