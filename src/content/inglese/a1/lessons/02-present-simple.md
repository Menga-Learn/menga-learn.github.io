## Present simple, *there is / there are* e lessico quotidiano

### Present simple

Si usa per abitudini, routine e fatti sempre veri: *I work in Milan*, *She drinks coffee every morning*.

- Alla **terza persona singolare** (he, she, it) il verbo prende **-s**: *he works*, *she lives*, *it opens*.
- Verbi in -o, -s, -sh, -ch, -x prendono **-es**: *goes*, *watches*, *washes*. *Have* diventa **has**.
- Consonante + y → **-ies**: *study → studies*.

**Negazioni e domande** usano l'ausiliare **do / does**, e il verbo torna alla forma base:

- *I **don't** like fish.* — *He **doesn't** work on Sunday.* (non ~~doesn't works~~)
- ***Do** you speak English?* — ***Does** she live here?* — *Where **do** they work?*

### Avverbi di frequenza

**always – usually – often – sometimes – never** vanno **prima del verbo principale** ma **dopo *to be***:
*I usually get up at 7.* — *She is always late.*

### There is / there are

Per dire che qualcosa "c'è":

- *There **is** a bank near the station.* (singolare)
- *There **are** two bedrooms in my flat.* (plurale)
- Domanda: *Is there a supermarket here?* — *Are there any chairs?*

### Preposizioni di tempo

- **at** + ora: *at 8 o'clock*, *at night*
- **on** + giorno: *on Monday*, *on 5 May*
- **in** + mese, anno, parti del giorno: *in July*, *in 2025*, *in the morning*

### Lessico essenziale A1

- Famiglia: *mother, father, sister, brother, son, daughter, husband, wife*
- Giorni: *Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday* (sempre con la maiuscola)
- Casa: *kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room*
- Cibo: *bread, milk, eggs, apple, chicken, rice, water*
- Lavoro: *office, job, boss, colleague, meeting*
