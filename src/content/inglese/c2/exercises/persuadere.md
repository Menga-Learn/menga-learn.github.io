## Difendi la posizione opposta

Scegli una delle affermazioni e difendi per **3 minuti** la posizione **contraria alla tua opinione reale**:

- *Homework should be abolished in primary schools.*
- *Artificial intelligence will create more jobs than it destroys.*
- *Cities should be allowed to limit the number of tourists.*

Poi rispondi in un minuto all'obiezione più forte che ti viene in mente. Registrati e trascrivi l'apertura, una concessione e la chiusura.

### Estratti modello (tema: limitare i turisti)

**Apertura**
*I'd like to start by pointing out that nobody is proposing to close cities to visitors. The question is a far narrower one: whether a city has the right to manage its own capacity, as any theatre or stadium does. Put that way, I'd argue the answer is not only yes, but that it's long overdue.*

**Concessione strategica**
*Now, you might reasonably object that tourism pays the bills — and in many places it does. I'm happy to concede that point, because it actually strengthens my case: if tourism is that valuable, it's all the more reason not to let it destroy the very thing people travel to see.*

**Risposta all'obiezione ("È elitario: solo i ricchi potranno viaggiare")**
*That's a fair concern, and I don't think it should be dismissed. Mind you, the current situation is hardly egalitarian either — it's the residents, often on modest incomes, who are being priced out of their own neighbourhoods. A well-designed system, say a free booking slot rather than a fee, would address precisely that.*

**Chiusura**
*So, the bottom line is this: managing numbers isn't about keeping people out. It's about making sure there's still a city worth visiting in twenty years' time.*

### Checklist

- Hai **ridefinito la questione** a tuo vantaggio nell'apertura?
- La concessione è **strategica** (la usi per rafforzare la tua tesi)?
- Hai usato marcatori naturali (*mind you, the bottom line, say*)?
- Le distinzioni sono precise (*not… but…*, *far narrower*)?
