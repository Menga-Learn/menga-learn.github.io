## Due email, due registri

Sei iscritto a un corso serale di fotografia. La scuola ti scrive che, da settembre, il corso passerà **dal martedì al sabato mattina** e costerà **il 20% in più**.

1. Scrivi un'email **informale** a un amico che frequenta il corso con te (circa **50 parole**): racconta la novità e cosa ne pensi.
2. Scrivi un'email **formale** alla direttrice della scuola, Ms Carter (**120–150 parole**): esprimi le tue preoccupazioni e fai una proposta.

<!-- SOLUZIONE -->

### Email informale (52 parole)

> Hi Luca,
> Have you seen the school's email? From September the course moves to Saturday mornings and it's going to cost 20% more! I can't do Saturdays because of my job at the market. I'm really disappointed. I'm going to write to them — do you want to sign it too?
> Cheers, Anna

### Email formale (137 parole)

> Dear Ms Carter,
> I am writing regarding the changes to the evening photography course announced in your email of 3 July.
> I have attended the course for two years and I have always found it extremely useful. However, I am concerned about the new arrangements. Firstly, many students, including myself, work on Saturday mornings and would therefore be unable to attend. Secondly, a 20% increase seems excessive, especially as no explanation has been given.
> I would like to suggest keeping at least one evening group on Tuesdays, perhaps at a slightly higher price for a smaller group. Alternatively, it might be possible to offer a discount to students who have already completed a year.
> I would be grateful if you could consider these proposals. I look forward to hearing from you.
> Yours sincerely,
> Anna Ferri

### Cosa controllare

- **Registro**: contrazioni e *Cheers* solo nella prima; nessuna contrazione e *Yours sincerely* (conosci il nome) nella seconda.
- **Struttura formale**: motivo → contesto → problemi (*Firstly, Secondly*) → proposta → richiesta → chiusura.
- **Tempi**: present perfect per l'esperienza (*I have attended… for two years*), *going to* per il cambiamento previsto.
