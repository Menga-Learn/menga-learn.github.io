## Confronta due foto

Immagina due foto:

- **Foto 1**: una famiglia fa un picnic in un parco, i bambini giocano con un pallone, il sole splende.
- **Foto 2**: una coppia è seduta in un ristorante elegante di città, di sera, con le candele sul tavolo.

Registrati (45 secondi per domanda):

1. *Tell me what you can see in the two pictures.*
2. *Which of these places would you prefer to spend a free afternoon in? Why?*
3. *Why do you think some people prefer to eat outdoors?*

Poi trascrivi la risposta alla domanda 1.

<!-- SOLUZIONE -->

### Risposte modello

1. *Both pictures show people eating together, but in very different situations. In the first one a family is having a picnic in a park. It's a sunny day and the children are playing with a ball, so it looks very relaxed and informal. **Whereas** the second picture shows a couple in an elegant restaurant in the evening. There are candles on the table, so it **might be** a special occasion, like an anniversary.*
2. *Personally, I'd prefer the park, because I love being outside when the weather is good. Restaurants are nice, but they can be expensive and a bit formal. In the park you can relax, play and take as long as you want.*
3. *I think people like eating outdoors because it makes them feel free. After a week in an office, being in nature is a way to relax. Also, a picnic is cheaper than a restaurant, which is important for families with children.*

### Checklist

- Hai detto cosa hanno **in comune** (*Both pictures…*) prima delle differenze?
- Hai usato almeno un connettivo di contrasto (*whereas, while, on the other hand*)?
- Hai **speculato** (*might be, looks like*) invece di dare per certo?
- Nella domanda 2 c'è **opinione → motivo → confronto**?
