## Discorso indiretto, passivo avanzato e lessico B2

### Reported speech

Quando riporti ciò che qualcuno ha detto con un verbo al passato (*said, told*), i tempi di solito **arretrano**:

| Diretto | Indiretto |
| --- | --- |
| "I **am** tired." | She said (that) she **was** tired. |
| "I **have finished**." | He said he **had finished**. |
| "I **will** call you." | She said she **would** call me. |
| "**Can** you help?" | He asked if I **could** help. |

- **say** senza persona, **tell** con la persona: *She said that…* / *She told **me** that…*
- Domande indirette: ordine **soggetto + verbo**, niente *do*: *He asked me where **I lived*** (non ~~where did I live~~).
- Verbi più precisi di *say*: *suggest, admit, deny, refuse, promise, warn, remind*. *She **suggested going** to the beach.* — *He **denied taking** the money.*

### Passivo avanzato e *causative*

- passivo con modali: *The form **must be signed**.*
- passivo impersonale: ***It is believed that** the castle was built in 1200.* / *The castle **is believed to have been** built…*
- **have something done**: far fare qualcosa a qualcun altro → *I **had my car repaired*** (non l'ho riparata io).

### Lessico: collocazioni e phrasal verb

Al B2 conta **quali parole vanno insieme**:

| Collocazione | Non… |
| --- | --- |
| **make** a decision, a mistake, progress | ~~do a decision~~ |
| **do** research, your best, a favour | ~~make research~~ |
| **take** a risk, part in, advantage of | |
| **heavy** traffic, **strong** coffee | ~~strong traffic~~ |

Phrasal verb frequenti: *carry out* (svolgere), *put off* (rimandare), *come up with* (ideare), *turn down* (rifiutare), *run out of* (finire), *look into* (esaminare), *set up* (avviare).

### Formazione delle parole

*decide → decision → decisive → indecisive*; *employ → employer/employee → unemployment*; prefissi negativi **un-, in-, im-, ir-, dis-**: *unlikely, inefficient, impossible, irrelevant, dishonest*.
