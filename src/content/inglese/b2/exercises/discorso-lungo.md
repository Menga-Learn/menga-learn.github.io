## Discorso lungo: due minuti su un tema astratto

Hai **1 minuto** per prendere appunti, poi parla per **2 minuti** rispondendo a tutte e tre le domande:

1. *Tell me about a piece of technology you couldn't live without.*
2. *How has it changed the way you live or work?*
3. *Do you think people today depend too much on technology? Why?*

Registrati, riascoltati e scrivi qui sotto i tuoi appunti e la trascrizione della parte che ti è venuta meglio.

### Appunti modello (1 minuto)

- smartphone — mappe, lavoro, famiglia lontana
- prima: cartine, telefono fisso → ora videochiamate con mia sorella a Londra; lavoro ovunque
- dipendenza? sì in parte: notifiche, sonno; ma dipende dall'uso → regole personali

### Risposta modello (circa 2 minuti)

*I'd like to talk about my smartphone, which, to be honest, I really couldn't live without. I use it for almost everything: maps, banking, work emails and, most importantly, keeping in touch with my family.*

*As for how it has changed my life, the biggest difference is probably with my sister, who moved to London five years ago. If we hadn't had video calls, I think we would have lost touch, but instead we talk almost every day. It has also changed the way I work: I can answer urgent messages from anywhere, which is convenient, although it means I'm never completely off duty.*

*Moving on to whether we depend too much on technology, I'd say that, to some extent, we do. Many people check their phones hundreds of times a day, and there's evidence that it affects sleep and concentration. However, I don't think technology itself is the problem — it's how we use it. Personally, I've started switching off notifications after nine in the evening, and it's made a real difference. So, overall, I'd say technology is a fantastic tool, as long as we stay in control of it.*

### Checklist

- Hai risposto a **tutte e tre** le domande, con passaggi segnalati (*As for…*, *Moving on to…*)?
- Hai usato almeno un **terzo condizionale** o una struttura complessa?
- Hai dato un'opinione **sfumata** (*to some extent*, *it depends on*)?
- Hai parlato per quasi tutti i due minuti senza lunghe pause?
