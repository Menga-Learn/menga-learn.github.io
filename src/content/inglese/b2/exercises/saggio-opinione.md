## Saggio di opinione: lavorare da remoto

Un sito di discussione chiede ai lettori:

> *Should companies allow employees to work from home permanently? Give reasons and examples.*

Scrivi la tua risposta in **180–220 parole**, con introduzione, due paragrafi di argomenti e conclusione. Usa almeno **tre connettivi diversi** e una frase che **concede** un punto all'opinione contraria.

<!-- SOLUZIONE -->

### Una risposta modello (205 parole)

> Since the pandemic, working from home has gone from being a rare privilege to an everyday reality for millions of people. In my view, companies should offer it permanently, although not necessarily every day.
>
> The most obvious advantage is time. Commuting can take up to two hours a day in big cities, and that time could be spent with family, exercising or simply resting. **Furthermore**, many employees report that they concentrate better at home, away from the constant interruptions of an open-plan office. A good example is my own team: since we started working remotely two days a week, our projects have been delivered faster.
>
> **Admittedly**, remote work is not ideal for everyone. Young employees, in particular, may miss the chance to learn by watching experienced colleagues, and some people feel isolated. **Nevertheless**, these problems can be solved by combining home and office days, rather than by forcing everyone back to their desks.
>
> **All things considered**, I believe that flexible arrangements benefit both workers and companies. Employers who refuse to offer them are likely to lose their best staff to competitors who do.

### Cosa notare

- **Posizione chiara** nell'introduzione e ribadita nella conclusione con parole diverse.
- **Concessione** (*Admittedly…*) seguita dalla risposta (*Nevertheless…*): è la mossa tipica del B2.
- **Esempio concreto** nel primo paragrafo.
- Strutture variate: present perfect (*has gone*), passivo (*can be solved*), *rather than*, *are likely to*.
