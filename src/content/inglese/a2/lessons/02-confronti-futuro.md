## Comparativi, quantità e futuro

### Comparativi e superlativi

| Aggettivo | Comparativo | Superlativo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cheap (1 sillaba) | cheap**er** | the cheap**est** |
| big | bi**gger** | the bi**ggest** |
| easy (-y) | eas**ier** | the eas**iest** |
| expensive (2+ sillabe) | **more** expensive | the **most** expensive |
| good | **better** | the **best** |
| bad | **worse** | the **worst** |
| far | **further / farther** | the **furthest** |

- Nel paragone si usa **than**: *Rome is bigger **than** Florence* (non ~~of~~).
- Uguaglianza: ***as** tall **as***; *not as expensive as*.

### Some, any, much, many

- **Numerabili** (si contano: *apples, chairs*): *how **many***, *a lot of*, *a few*.
- **Non numerabili** (*water, money, information, bread*): *how **much***, *a lot of*, *a little*.
- **some** nelle frasi affermative e nelle offerte (*Would you like some tea?*); **any** in negative e domande (*Have you got any brothers?*).
- *Information*, *advice*, *furniture*, *news* sono **non numerabili** in inglese: ~~informations~~.

### Parlare del futuro

- **be going to** + verbo: piani e intenzioni già decisi → *I'm going to visit my aunt next week*.
- **present continuous**: appuntamenti fissati → *I'm meeting Sara at 6 tomorrow*.
- **will**: decisioni prese sul momento, promesse, previsioni generiche → *It's cold. I'll close the window.*

### Obbligo e possibilità

- **can / can't**: capacità e permesso → *Can I sit here?*
- **have to**: obbligo esterno (regole) → *I have to wear a uniform at work*.
- **must / mustn't**: regole forti, divieti → *You mustn't smoke here*.
- **don't have to**: non è necessario (≠ *mustn't*!) → *You don't have to come* = non sei obbligato.
