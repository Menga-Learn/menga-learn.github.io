## Descrivi una foto in 45 secondi

Immagina questa foto: *un mercato all'aperto; in primo piano una donna anziana compra frutta da un venditore sorridente; sullo sfondo ci sono altre bancarelle e molte persone; il cielo è nuvoloso.*

Registrati mentre rispondi a queste tre richieste (45 secondi ciascuna):

1. *Describe this picture.*
2. *Tell me about the last time you went shopping for food.*
3. *Do you prefer shopping at markets or in supermarkets? Why?*

Poi scrivi qui sotto la trascrizione della risposta migliore.

<!-- SOLUZIONE -->

### Risposte modello

1. *In this picture I can see an outdoor market. In the foreground an old woman is buying some fruit. The man who is selling the fruit is smiling. In the background there are a lot of people and other stalls. The sky is grey, so maybe it's autumn.*
2. *I went food shopping last Saturday. I went to the supermarket near my house with my husband. We bought vegetables, pasta and some meat for the week. It was very busy, so we waited a long time at the checkout.*
3. *I prefer markets because the fruit and vegetables are fresher and cheaper. I also like talking to the people who work there. But supermarkets are more practical when I'm busy.*

### Checklist

- Nella descrizione hai usato il **present continuous** (*is buying, is smiling*)?
- Hai usato **in the foreground / in the background**?
- Nella seconda risposta sei rimasto al **past simple** (*went, bought, waited*)?
- Nella terza hai dato **opinione + motivo + (eventuale) contrasto**?
